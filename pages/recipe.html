Lemon ricotta pasta

A little tip

Save a mug of pasta water. It’s the secret to a silky sauce.

Let’s make it

  1. Get the pasta going

    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the rigatoni according to the package directions. Add the peas for the final 2 minutes. Reserve 250 ml of pasta water, then drain.

  2. Make the lemon ricotta sauce

    In a large bowl, whisk together the ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil and Parmesan. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.

  3. Bring it all together

    Add the hot pasta, peas and spinach to the sauce. Toss gently, adding the reserved pasta water a little at a time until silky. The heat will wilt the spinach.

  4. Finish and enjoy

    Divide between warm bowls. Top with fresh basil, extra Parmesan and a little more lemon zest. Serve straight away.
